The Baker's Dozen delved into the ruins of a dwarven city far beneath the streets of Capitol to make an offering to a statue of the Dwarven God of Ale, Trim.

Three days pass. With no public progress toward finding the criminals responsible for stealing from the gnomish tribute, the Emperor's patrols begin to wane.

**Ryan Wythyneye's Job**

With the Rosencrans absent from Capitol and traffic to their warehouses dwindling, Dock Street's leaders are looking to expand. To make it work, they need legitimacy, and that means deeds.

If the original deed to the main warehouse can be found, they offer 160 g.p. A bonus 50 g.p. would be paid for deeds to the other two warehouses. No one can know they sponsored the crime, and it must be done by dawn.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Dock Street and the underworld - Rep: Capitol's nobility

**Tiny Tim's Job**

The Guard has long suspected the Sorceress Miksa Nikoletta of involvement in the illegal drug trade. They have suspicions but no evidence, and moving without it could be disastrous.

They offer 200 g.p. to the crew that can break in and find the evidence. Leave no trace, and make sure no one knows they were there.

The job must be done in 48 hours. After that, the Gray Cloaks will be released from patrol duty.

Reputation Yield + Rep: The Guard and the Faith - Rep: The Imperial College of Arcane Mysteries
