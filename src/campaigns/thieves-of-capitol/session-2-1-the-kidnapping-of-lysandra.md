Lysandra, the 15-year-old daughter of Arch Cleric Astora, has been kidnapped by river pirates. They have demanded a 5,000 g.p. ransom, but Astora can only scrape together 500 g.p. She has asked the Baker's Dozen to solve the problem, offering the 500 g.p. as a reward.

The exchange is set to take place at the Belaying Pin tomorrow at 8:00 PM, giving the crew only 26 hours to find the girl before the ransom is due.
