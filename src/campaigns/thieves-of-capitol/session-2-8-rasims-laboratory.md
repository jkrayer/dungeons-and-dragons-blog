Deep in the sewers, in a gray dimension, the Baker's Dozen interrupts Rasim Usak's lethal and obscene experiments.

**Skinny Pete's Job**

Only twelve hours of game time have passed since Pete's earlier offer to eliminate Archie O'Tierney. Thirty-six hours remain on the contract.

Pete repeats the same terms: remove O'Tierney within 48 hours, preferably in a public and bloody fashion. Pay is 200 g.p. each, plus a 100 g.p. each bonus if done in the guild hall during daylight.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Fledgling Thieves' Guild - Rep: Porters Guild

**Kobblestone's Job (Una)**

With business still slow, Una keeps the bakery open. Around mid-morning, three diminutive figures arrive. Their earthen scent betrays them as gnomes. The leader introduces himself as Angus Kobblestone, a free gnome.

He asks the crew to strike a blow against tyranny: infiltrate the Imperial Palace, map the vault and its defenses, and leave no trace.

Angus offers 230 g.p.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Kobblestone's Killers - Rep: None if done without a trace
