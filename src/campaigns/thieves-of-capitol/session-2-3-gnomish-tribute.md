The Baker's Dozen has learned of the gnomish tribute's arrival in the Empire. It is one of the most heavily guarded treasures to arrive in Capitol, but also a literal king's ransom.

**Tiny Tim's Job**

A rumor is circulating that Anton Rosencrans is sneaking back into the city tomorrow night, and someone has put a price on his head.

An unknown benefactor offers 1,000 g.p. if he can be captured.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Underworld, Nobility, Faith, Authorities and the benefactor - Rep: The Veiled

**Ryan Wythyneye's Job**

Left-Eyed Loukas, a stern-looking fellow with the bearing of a soldier, is looking for a crew to get him into Arch-Wizard Kostas Kleidemos's home. He wants to steal an item from the home but will not identify it. He is insistent that the job be done without leaving any trace of the theft.

Loukas is shopping around for a crew, so speed matters. If completed in the next 48 hours, the Baker's Dozen can beat rival crews to the job.

Pay is 230 g.p. for each member of the crew.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Unknown benefactor - Rep: The Imperial College of Arcane Mysteries and Kostas Kleidemos
