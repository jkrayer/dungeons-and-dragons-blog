"Cutter's" corpse successfully recovered, the dark spirit Ikoros commanded the Baker's Dozen to recover the scroll of Grazz't. With that task completed, Ikoros now commands the crew to track down the sorcerer Rasim Usak.

Ikoros calls this next work urgent, dangerous and lucrative. Deep in the sewers, Rasim Usak is distilling obscene tonics for an unknown benefactor, and one such concoction nears completion.

The Baker's Dozen must find the laboratory, steal the eldritch tonic, gather all evidence regarding Rasim's patron, and destroy the place before dawn.

Ikoros provides a map and offers 300 g.p.

Reputation Yield + Rep: The Rebel Alchemists - Rep: None if the crew succeeds
