The Baker's Dozen has been hired to break into House Kleidemos to recover a hunting trophy for their client, Thefri Hercle. Thefri is anxious for its return and may go ahead without them, costing a valuable payday.

**Gordon's Job**

All anyone drinking at Hammer Hall can talk about is the annual gnomish tribute from Whistleport: three trade vessels packed with precious and semi-precious gems and stones.

The delivery is one of the most tightly kept secrets in Capitol. No one knows exactly when it comes or on what ships, but if the rumors are true the treasure will arrive in two days on the Bellmont, Warren and Regent.

The tribute will arrive under guard of the feared 4th Legion, and it is said it will be greeted by the Imperial Guard, but the road from port to treasury is long.

If successful, each member of the Baker's Dozen could carry away 2,500 g.p.
