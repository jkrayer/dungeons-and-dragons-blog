Early the following day a great clap of thunder rattles timber building for miles around and shatters nearby windows. Fantastic rumors of a ground level thunder strike fly through the city faster than wildfire.

Tracing the rumors back to the source you find a smoldering crater where last night's forge once stood. Many more surrounding buildings are flattened or leaning away, indicating a blast of unimaginable power centered on the forge.
