The Baker's Dozen has learned that Anton Rosencrans is planning to sneak back into the city. They seek the time and location of his arrival.

**The Party's Job**

Sewer Rats have been disappearing in unusual places: not near known hazards, but in mundane tunnels under the city. No bodies have been found. Their numbers simply dwindle while friends disappear, leaving worldly possessions behind.

The Rats offer 100 g.p. to each member of the crew and need this done before more go missing.

Reputation Yield + Rep: The Sewer Rats - Rep: Unknown

**Ovys the Oracular**

Ovys the Oracular, sometime advisor to House Tantalas, seeks the crew out after a recommendation from Alexandar. He needs a task completed with the utmost discretion, with details to be disclosed in person.

He offers 100 g.p. for each member of the crew and a magical item.

Reputation Yield + Rep: Ovys the Oracular and the Imperial College of Arcane Mysteries - Rep: Unknown
