Click by clack the adventurers are lowered into the earth&hellip;

In the pit-city Kartusch the adventurers meet Balsa the Wise who sends them, as she once sent Fuller, to the shattered city of Spinel. Finding nothing but Fuller's journal they return to Kartusch.

>"Devil bite my scrotum, another damned failure. Instead of granting me the immortality I so crave, the machine spat out a useless copy of my aging body. Useless, utterly useless. Thousands of lives sacrificed, for nil. Not that I care, but it was an exhausting exercise. Anyway, off to the corpse golem makers now. Maybe they know more about eternal life than they let on."
